The PAI Lab

The PAI Lab at the Institute of AI for Industries, Chinese Academy of Sciences, focuses on physical and spatial intelligence, world models, neural simulation, embodied intelligence, and robotic manipulation and navigation.

Our faculty work on the foundations and applications of physical and spatial intelligence.

Research Interests

Physical and spatial intelligence · world models · neural simulation · embodied intelligence · robotic manipulation and navigation.

The iGRAPE Lab

A one-sentence summary of our goal: we try to close the loop of acquisition, processing, understanding and generation of 3D geometric data with a data-driven approach. See this survey for the concept of the "data-driven loop".

We are recruiting. We are actively seeking self-motivated and well-trained students. If you are interested, email kevin.kai.xu (at) gmail.com.
