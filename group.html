The iGRAPE Lab

The iGRAPE Lab (Intelligent GRaphics And PErception) targets top-tier graphics and vision research at the School of Computer, National University of Defense Technology. Our interests span geometry processing and modeling, 3D vision, and robotic manipulation and navigation — with an emphasis on learning-based approaches.

A one-sentence summary of our goal: we try to close the loop of acquisition, processing, understanding and generation of 3D geometric data with a data-driven approach. See this survey for the concept of the "data-driven loop".

We are recruiting. We are actively seeking self-motivated and well-trained students. If you are interested, email kevin.kai.xu (at) gmail.com.
Research Interests
